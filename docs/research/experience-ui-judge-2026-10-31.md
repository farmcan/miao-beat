## P1 blocker: portrait performance inputs exceed viewport

Reproduction: actual DOM 390×844 → ordinary piano → click `全屏演奏`.

- The complete cat remains visible and the exit control works.
- Actual DOM read: `#playingSurface.getBoundingClientRect().width === 640` while `innerWidth === 390`.
- `.melody-scroll` also expands to 640px. `#keyboardNavigation` computed display becomes `none`, because it sees the expanded scroll region instead of a constrained viewport.
- Screenshot shows right-side piano keys and drum pads cut off by the screen. It is not just the intentional scrollable inner keyboard: the scroll container itself exceeds the visible surface, and the explicit low/high recovery navigation disappears.
- In the narrower preliminary actual 354×767 run, instrument buttons also collapsed to a narrow group with wrapped labels. This supports checking flex-item sizing together rather than applying another outer overflow mask.

Required acceptance after repair: at actual 390×844 portrait performance mode, the playing-surface/scroll viewport must remain within the screen; low/high navigation must remain available whenever the keyboard extends beyond that scroll viewport; high C5 and every drum voice must be reachable without horizontal page clipping. Recheck landscape and exit after changing portrait sizing.

## Up to three useful next improvements

1. **Prioritize the selected mode's main action on phones.** After choosing Practice or Record, the tall metronome currently appears before song selection / Start Recording. Put the selected mode's primary control first and make detailed metronome settings collapsible. Success: selecting Record brings a visible Start Recording action into the next view, and selecting Practice reveals song plus first-phrase action without crossing the full metronome block.
2. **Keep active transport visible while playing.** In ordinary phone practice the Stop control lives far below the guide/keys, requiring a scroll away from performance. A compact Stop / recording elapsed / stop-recording bar near the guide would reduce recovery effort. Test with phrase looping and recording, while preserving free manual inputs and avoiding automatic recorded notes.
3. **Make phrase choices identifiable.** Current `第 1 句`, `第 2 句` choices are functional but do not tell the player what they contain. Where available, add a short lyric or starting-note preview while preserving the numerical phrase index. This helps resume the intended passage without auditioning every option.

Do not use this review to restart 3D sourcing, crop production cat frames, or declare continuous animation solved. The reviewed candidate retains the orange-cat complete-image route.
